# User Guide

## Student

1. Open the home page and choose Register.
2. Enter your name, email, password and available academic details.
3. Log in and open Profile to update your details.
4. Open Jobs to review company, position and eligibility information.
5. Apply to an eligible job.
6. Open My Applications to review the current status.

## Administrator

1. Create the administrator account using the local setup instructions.
2. Log in with the administrator credentials.
3. Add company details from Companies.
4. Create job postings from the dashboard.
5. Review applications and update statuses from Applications.

## Application statuses

The starter accepts: `Submitted`, `Under Review`, `Shortlisted`, `Selected` and `Rejected`.

Use demo/test data while developing. Do not enter real student information until your institution has approved the privacy, access-control and data-retention arrangements.
